About this tracker

Herd Signal curates publicly reported AI/ML developments across the dairy value chain — from peer-reviewed research and preprints to industry press, cooperative platforms and conference programs. Entries with a specific report date show the full date; entries known only to the month are shown as "Mon YYYY." Day/Yesterday filtering only surfaces entries with a confirmed date.
