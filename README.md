# Herd Signal

A live-tracked, function-by-function map of AI and ML developments across the global dairy industry — animal health, breeding & genetics, nutrition & feeding, engineering & automation, quality & food safety, product development, sustainability & traceability, and digital platforms.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages on this repo to view it live. Filter the feed by day, week, month, or year, by function, or by country/region. Click any card to zoom into full details.

Sourced from research papers, industry press, and conference programs; each entry links back to its original source.
